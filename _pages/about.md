<p style="font-size: 14px;">Hi, I am an Associate Professor in the College of Design and Innovation at Tongji University. I am a core member of the Center of Digital Innovation (CDI), the Tongji-MIT City Science Lab@Shanghai (CSL), and I am also affiliated with the Institute for Sensing and Navigation (ISN) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.</p>

<p style="font-size: 14px;">I received my B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in Information and Communication Engineering from the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT), under the supervision of Prof. Wenxian Yu and Prof. Weidong Hu. I was also a joint Ph.D. student in the Multimedia and Vision (MMV) Group at Queen Mary University of London, working with Prof. Ioannis Patras. After completing my Ph.D., I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University with Prof. Wenxian Yu. I was honored as a Rising Star by ACM China SIGAI.</p>

<span class="highlight">Perception, Interaction and Design for People and Our Planet</span>

Our π (Pi) Group combines innovations in artificial intelligence and human-centered design to address real-world challenges—from individuals to the planet. Our long-term goal is to empower computers, robots, and agents to act intelligently and adaptively in complex and uncertain environments, while enhancing human well-being and experience through advances in both technology and design.
